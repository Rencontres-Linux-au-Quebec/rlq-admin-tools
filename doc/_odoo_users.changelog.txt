## 1.0.20261016.114848 — First version: register of banned and watched Odoo accounts

Reason:
  "[0] When registering for an event in Odoo, validate that people wrote a valid e-mail (example: mariebc33@gmail'com); add a menu option to list the e-mail errors caused by a wrong address, fix the user's contact and resend the e-mail. [1] In app-log-live.py, show what causes the most latency (SATA, not SSD, with ZFS on Proxmox): % used for I/O over several hours, to decide whether SSD are justified. [2] A program to run on the Proxmox server that details what causes performance problems (CPU, MEM, IO, NET) and suggests settings (ZFS…), light on resources. [3] Alignment problem with Chinese characters in web-monitor.py --list-users --table; a new account (Chinese name) may be a hacker: add a section for suspicious activity of a logged-in Odoo user — do not always trust them. [4] List the suspicious users, offer to investigate all the IPs of a user and blacklist them so they cannot log in to Odoo any more: --ban-odoo-user and --unban-odoo-user, but investigate more before banning (search the e-mail on the Internet, etc.)."

Changes:
  - record(), events(), state(), banned_logins(), watched_logins(): one tab-separated line per event (date, banned / unbanned / watched / unwatched,
    database, UID, login, addresses, reason) in /var/lib/nginx-tools/odoo-users.tsv (NGINX_TOOLS_ODOO_USERS; "off" disables it); the LAST event of an
    account decides its state; the history is never rewritten.
  - Read by web-monitor.py (summary, ban, watch), nginx-log-audit.sh (awk) and app-log-live.py (marks).

  none.
